Welcome

Forgot your passphrase?

Shrimpt cannot reset or recover it for you.

Why we can’t recover it

Your passphrase unlocks encrypted keys stored only on this device. It is never saved in plain text, so there is nothing to look up or reset.

If you have a backup

Remove Shrimpt, install it again, then use Settings → Backup → Restore with your backup file and password.

If you don’t have a backup

Keys and contacts on this device are lost. Remove the extension, reinstall, create a new identity, and share fresh handshakes with your contacts.
